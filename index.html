HomeStock Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · App: HomeStock (com.auhoolabs.homestock) · Developer: Yijian Hu

Summary

HomeStock does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no network permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The items and locations you record (names, quantities, units, tags, notes, barcodes), the photos you add to items, and the history of changes are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We have no access to them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently.

Camera

The camera is used to scan QR codes and barcodes while a scan screen is open, and to take a photo of an item when you choose to. Recognition happens on the device. Scan images are not saved; photos you take are saved only in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can also choose an existing photo with the Android photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. You can deny or revoke camera permission at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Printing labels

When you choose to print or share a label, the app creates a PDF and hands it to the Android print or share system. Where it goes from there is decided by you and the printer or app you pick.

Backups and exports

When you choose to save a backup or export your item list, the app creates a file (a backup contains all items, locations, history and photos; the list is a CSV file) and hands it to the Android save screen. You decide where it is saved, for example on the device or in a cloud storage app, whose terms then apply to that file. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Restoring a backup replaces the data in the app.

No network access, analytics or advertising

The app does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no accounts or sign-in.

Children

HomeStock is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

HomeStock 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日 · 应用：HomeStock（com.auhoolabs.homestock）· 开发者：Yijian Hu

概要

HomeStock 不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。你输入的所有内容都只保存在你的设备上。应用没有网络权限，无法向任何地方发送数据。

保存在设备上的数据

你记录的物品和位置信息（名称、数量、单位、标签、备注、条码）、为物品添加的照片以及变更历史，只保存在设备上应用的私有存储中，我们无法访问。卸载应用或清除应用数据会永久删除这些内容。

相机

相机用于在扫码页面识别二维码和条码，以及在你选择时为物品拍照。识别在设备上完成。扫码画面不会被保存；你拍的照片只保存在应用的私有存储中。任何内容都不会被发送出去。你也可以通过 Android 照片选择器选择已有照片，应用只能获得你选中的照片。你可以随时拒绝或撤销相机权限，应用的其他功能不受影响。

打印标签

当你选择打印或分享标签时，应用会生成 PDF 并交给 Android 系统的打印或分享功能。之后文件的去向由你以及你选择的打印机或应用决定。

备份与导出

当你选择保存备份或导出物品清单时，应用会生成一个文件（备份包含全部物品、位置、历史记录和照片；清单为 CSV 文件），并交给 Android 系统的保存界面。文件保存在哪里由你决定，例如保存在设备上或网盘应用中，此时该文件适用相应应用的条款。备份文件没有加密，请保存在你信任的地方。从备份恢复会替换应用中的数据。

无网络访问、无统计、无广告

应用不申请网络访问权限，不包含广告、统计分析、崩溃上报，也没有账号或登录。

儿童

HomeStock 是面向一般用户的工具，并非针对儿童设计。由于不收集任何数据，因此也不会收集儿童的数据。

变更

如果将来的版本加入以不同方式处理数据的功能（例如云同步），本政策会在该版本发布前更新，并修改上方的生效日期。
